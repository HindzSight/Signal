How it works

Four steps. Nothing to install for them.

Your recipient just clicks a link. Signal is the only thing that runs on your machine.

01

Choose a folder

Open Signal, browse to the folder you want to share. Nothing uploads yet.

02

Open a channel

Signal spins up a temporary Cloudflare tunnel and a random passcode, no server of yours involved.

03

Send the link

Share the link and passcode separately. Your recipient enters the passcode and browses the folder.

04

Watch it live

Every download shows up in real time: progress, speed, completion, right on your dashboard.

Why Signal

Built for "I just need to send this," not a storage product.

Files never leave your disk

Signal doesn't upload anything anywhere. It streams bytes directly from your folder to your recipient through the tunnel, with no intermediate storage and no size cap.

Live download telemetry

Watch each file's progress, speed, and status update in real time as your recipient downloads. No guessing whether it went through.

Passcode-protected, expiring links

Every share gets a random passcode and an auto-close timer (1 hour to 7 days). Stop it instantly whenever you want.

Whole folders, zipped on demand

Recipients can grab a single file, a multi-file selection, or the entire folder as one streamed zip, so no partial downloads get lost to browser limits.

Nothing to configure

cloudflared ships bundled inside the desktop app. Download, open, pick a folder, and you're transmitting in under a minute.

Free and open source
